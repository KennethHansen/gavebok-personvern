Privacy policy for Gavebok

Publisher: Bitsoft · Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Norsk versjon

Gavebok is an app for keeping track of the gifts a family gives and receives. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it is stored.

In short

By default, everything you enter in Gavebok is stored only on your own device, and Bitsoft has no access to it. If you choose to share with your family, you sign in and the family's data is stored in the cloud (Google Firebase, EU) so everyone in the family sees the same. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

What the app stores

The information you enter yourself: names of people and groups, gifts (date, giver, recipient, description, amount or value, occasion and note), special days such as birthdays, and gift ideas. The app also stores simple settings, such as your language, currency, when you last made a backup and when you want reminders.

Without sharing, the information is stored in a database in the app's private storage on the device and is not sent over the internet by the app.

Family sharing (optional)

When you sign in and create or join a family, Bitsoft processes the following to provide sharing:

  • Account: name, email address and user ID from Google or Apple, which you sign in with.
  • The family: the family's name, who the members are, and temporary invite codes.
  • Content: people, gifts, special days, gift ideas, occasions and currency that the family records.

Bitsoft is the data controller. The legal basis is the contract to provide the service you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). The information is stored with Google Firebase as data processor. The family's content is stored in the EU. The sign-in service (Firebase Authentication) may process account information in the USA, based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Only members of the family can access the family's data. The information is encrypted in transit. The app keeps a local copy on each device so it works offline.

The information is kept as long as you are a member. If you leave the family or delete your account, your membership is removed. When the last member leaves, the family's data in the cloud is deleted. A copy remains on your device.

Backup and export

When you make a backup or export to a spreadsheet, a file is created that you choose to save or share, for example in a cloud service or by email. Where the file ends up and who can access it is up to you. Bitsoft does not receive the file.

On Android, the system's built-in backup may store a copy of the app's data in your Google account if you have turned on backup in the phone's settings. This is controlled by you and Google, and Bitsoft has no access to this copy.

Reminders

Reminders about special days are created and shown locally on the device. The app asks for permission to show notifications, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in the phone's settings.

Sharing with others

Gavebok does not sell or share information with third parties. If you use family sharing, the information is processed by Google Firebase on behalf of Bitsoft, as described above.

Children

The app is made for adults. Information about children in the app is entered by parents or other adults, and is shared with the family only if they choose to.

Deletion

You can delete individual gifts, people, special days and ideas in the app. If you uninstall the app, all information is deleted from the device. Backup files you have saved elsewhere must be deleted there.

If you have an account, you can delete it in the app under Settings → Family sharing → Delete account. You can also request deletion by emailing post@bitsoft.no. You have the right to access, rectification and erasure, and may complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If the app changes so that it handles information differently, this policy and the date at the top will be updated.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Gavebok can be sent to Bitsoft: post@bitsoft.no.
